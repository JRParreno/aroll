# Aroll+ Biometric Consent

This consent covers only face checks used for workplace attendance. It is separate from the Terms, the Privacy Policy, and your phone’s camera or location prompts.

Agreeing in the app records this consent and the document version. Complete it before face enrollment.

---

## 1. Plain-language summary

Your employer may use Aroll+ face attendance to confirm it is you clocking in.

1. During **enrollment**, the system takes face photos and turns them into a number code of your face (a template).
2. During **Time In / Time Out**, the app may take your face photo, check that you are a real person, check workplace location, and compare the photo to your enrolled template.
3. Raw photos are processed to create templates. Aroll+ is not designed to keep a long-term photo album.
4. This is **not** Apple Face ID. Templates are stored in the Aroll+ database for this business and protected by ordinary app security (sign-in, access control, and HTTPS in deployed environments).

---

## 2. Purpose

Face data is used only to:

- Enroll your face for workplace attendance checks
- Confirm it is you when clocking in and out
- Support the attendance record your employer uses for timekeeping and payroll

It is not used for marketing, sold to third parties, or shared with other businesses on Aroll+.

---

## 3. Data covered

- Face photos during enrollment and face attendance
- Face template(s) stored for matching
- Results of the live-person check and face match, linked to attendance
- Location coordinates at Time In / Time Out for a workplace check, when that feature is turned on

---

## 4. If you decline or withdraw

You may decline or later withdraw this consent in the app.

- Face enrollment and face Time In / Time Out become unavailable.
- Stored face templates for your account are cleared.
- Other workplace features that do not need face recognition stay available.

This consent does not decide employment outcomes. Those remain with your employer and applicable labor rules.

---

## 5. Limits

- Face matching can fail in poor light, unusual angles, or lookalike cases and may need a retry or re-enrollment.
- The live-person check reduces still-photo spoofing but may not stop every advanced attack.
- No system can promise zero risk of unauthorized access.

---

## 6. Phone permissions

Camera access is requested when you enroll or use face attendance. Location while using the app may be requested for the workplace check. Those phone permissions are not this consent. You can turn them off in your phone settings.
