## 2. What Aroll+ does

Aroll+ may be used to:

- Register a business and review owner details
- Enroll employees and assign shifts
- Record Time In and Time Out using face recognition and workplace location checks
- Support leave requests, notifications, and payroll calculations based on recorded attendance

Features available to you depend on your role and on how the business was set up.

## 3. Accounts and access

You must keep your sign-in details confidential and use only the account issued to you.

Owners are responsible for the accuracy of business information, employee records, schedules, workplace location, and payroll configuration they enter.

Employees are responsible for using Time In / Time Out only for their own attendance.

## 4. Face recognition and location

Live (non-demo) employee attendance may require:

- Camera access, to capture a face image for identity matching
- Location access, to check that attendance is recorded near the saved workplace geofence

Demo accounts use a seeded identity shortcut and fictional worksite coordinates. Demo mode does not open the camera or request the phone’s GPS for attendance.

Face matching uses the existing Aroll+ recognition pipeline. Raw enrollment photos are processed into numeric embeddings and are not kept as a long-term photo gallery.

## 5. Attendance and payroll records

Attendance, leave, and payroll figures shown in Aroll+ are generated from data entered or captured in the system. Owners should review records before treating them as final for pay or discipline.

Aroll+ is a prototype. Do not use prototype or demo data as the sole basis for employment decisions, discipline, or actual salary payment unless your research protocol expressly allows it.
