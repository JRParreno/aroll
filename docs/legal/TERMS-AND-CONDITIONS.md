# Aroll+ Terms and Conditions

These terms explain how you may use Aroll+ for workplace attendance, schedules, leave, overtime, and payroll.

Agreeing in the app records that you have read this document. If you do not agree, do not continue into the app.

---

## 1. What Aroll+ is

Aroll+ helps businesses record attendance and prepare payroll. It may include:

- Sign-in for owners, managers, employees, and platform administrators
- Employee accounts created by the owner or manager
- Shift schedules
- Time In / Time Out, including a face check, a check that you are a real person (not a photo), and a workplace location check when those features are turned on
- Leave requests, overtime review, and attendance correction requests
- Attendance records and payslip viewing
- Business sign-up review by a platform administrator

Aroll+ helps record time and prepare payroll. It does not replace the employer’s duty to review pay and follow labor rules.

---

## 2. Who may use the system

- **Platform administrator:** reviews new businesses and runs the service.
- **Business owner / manager:** looks after the workforce, enrollment, attendance, and payroll for that business.
- **Employee:** uses an assigned account to view schedule, attendance, and payslips, and to clock in or out as required by the employer.

You may use only the account given to you. Employees cannot create their own account.

---

## 3. Accounts and security

- Keep your password private. Do not share your login.
- Change a temporary password when the app asks you to.
- Tell your employer if you think someone else used your account.
- Your employer or a platform administrator may turn off an account when employment ends or when access is no longer allowed.

You are responsible for actions taken under your account.

---

## 4. Acceptable use

You agree not to:

- Clock in or out for another person
- Fake your location, face check, or the check that you are a real person
- Look at another business’s data or another employee’s records without permission
- Disrupt or overload the service beyond normal use
- Upload unlawful or fake sign-up documents
- Use Aroll+ for anything other than work attendance and related HR or payroll tasks at the business that uses it

---

## 5. Attendance, leave, overtime, and pay

- Record attendance only for yourself.
- Use leave, overtime, and correction features only for your own records. Approvals follow the process this workplace set.
- Face enrollment and face Time In / Time Out need a separate biometric consent.
- The employer is still responsible for workplace rules and for checking payroll before paying you.

---

## 6. Phone permissions

The mobile app may ask your phone for:

- **Camera:** face enrollment and face Time In / Time Out. Not used in the background.
- **Location while using the app:** a check that you are at work at Time In / Time Out. Aroll+ does not keep tracking your location.
- **Notifications:** attendance and account alerts. You can skip this and still use other features.

These phone prompts are not the same as agreeing to these terms or to the Privacy Policy. You can change or turn a permission off later in your phone settings. Turning one off only stops the feature that needs it.

- You are allowed to operate this business on the platform.
- Sign-up details you submit are accurate to the best of your knowledge.
- You will use employee data only for legitimate employment and payroll purposes.
- You will not export or share employee face templates outside Aroll+ without a lawful reason.

## 9. Accuracy limits

Face recognition may fail in poor light, unusual angles, or attempted spoofing. Payroll amounts come from the workplace’s rules and recorded attendance. Always review payroll before payment.

Personal information is described in the Privacy Policy. Using Aroll+ means you have been shown that notice.
